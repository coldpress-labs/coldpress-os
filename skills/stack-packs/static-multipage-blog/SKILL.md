## Purpose

The `static-multipage-blog` pack provides a cohesive starting stack for content-heavy sites, personal blogs, and marketing sites that need a blog. SEO/AEO/LLM, accessibility, and future-proof baselines are covered out-of-box.

## Pack Contents

| Skill | Phase | Purpose |
|-------|-------|---------|
| [quickstart](quickstart/) | 3 | Scaffold Astro + Vercel + MDX + Tailwind + RSS + sitemap + blog post template |

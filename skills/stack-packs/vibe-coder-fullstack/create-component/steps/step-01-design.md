## Goal

Understand what the component needs and design the data model.

## Instructions

1. **Gather requirements** — What does this component do?
2. **Design data model** — Tables, fields, relationships, indexes.
3. **Identify functions needed** — Queries, mutations, actions.
4. **Plan real-time behavior** — What updates in real-time?
5. **Present design** to user.

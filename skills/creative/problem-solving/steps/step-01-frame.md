## Goal

Ensure we're solving the right problem before looking for solutions.

## Instructions

1. **State the problem** in one clear sentence.
2. **Define scope** — what's in bounds and out of bounds?
3. **Identify constraints** — time, budget, technology, organizational.
4. **Decompose** — break the problem into sub-problems if complex.
5. **Validate** — "Is this the actual problem, or a symptom of something deeper?"

## User Interaction

Guide through framing questions. Confirm the problem statement.

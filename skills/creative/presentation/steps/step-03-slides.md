## Goal

Plan each slide following the narrative arc.

## Instructions

For each slide, define:
1. **Title** — concise and compelling
2. **Key point** — one idea per slide
3. **Visual direction** — chart type, image concept, diagram, or text layout
4. **Data** — specific numbers, quotes, or evidence to include
5. **Transition** — how it connects to the next slide

Apply the rule: **if a slide doesn't advance the narrative, cut it.**

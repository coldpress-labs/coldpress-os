## Goal

Understand who we're talking to, what we need to convey, and why.

## Instructions

1. **Identify audience** — Who will hear/read this? What do they care about?
2. **Define core message** — What's the one thing they should take away?
3. **Clarify purpose** — Inspire, persuade, educate, or entertain?
4. **Gather context** — What does the audience already know? What are their objections?

## User Interaction

Guide through context questions. Confirm audience and message.

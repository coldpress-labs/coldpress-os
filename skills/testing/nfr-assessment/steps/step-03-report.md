## Goal

Compile assessment results into a clear report with actionable next steps.

## Instructions

1. **Build gap analysis matrix:**

   | NFR | Category | Status | Risk | Evidence | Remediation |
   |-----|----------|--------|------|----------|-------------|

2. **Prioritize remediations** by risk (Critical first).
3. **Estimate effort** for each remediation (S/M/L).
4. **Write report** to output location.
5. **Present summary** to user.

## User Interaction

Present overall NFR health score and top priority remediations.

## Output

NFR assessment report. Workflow complete.

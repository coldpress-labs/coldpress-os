## Router

This is a cross-cutting creative router. It is available during Phase 4 (Planning) but is not Phase 4-owned — it can be invoked across phases 2 through 8.

-> Read and follow `../../skills/creative/problem-solving/SKILL.md`

## Phase 4 Context

During Phase 4 planning, problem-solving is useful for:
- Resolving conflicting product requirements or design constraints
- Breaking down ambiguous requirements into concrete specifications
- Navigating scope decisions when features compete for priority
- Finding solutions to feasibility challenges identified during PRD authoring

## Phase 4 Context

During planning, problem-solving is used to:
- Resolve conflicting product requirements or design constraints
- Work through complex technical architecture trade-offs
- Break down ambiguous requirements into concrete specifications
- Navigate scope decisions when features compete for priority
- Find creative solutions to feasibility challenges identified during planning

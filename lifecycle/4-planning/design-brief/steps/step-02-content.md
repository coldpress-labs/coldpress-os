## Goal

Establish the content strategy, brand voice, and messaging framework that will guide all copy and content across the product.

## Instructions

1. **Brand voice:**
   - "How should this product sound?" (professional, playful, authoritative, warm, etc.)
   - Define 3-5 voice attributes with examples
   - Identify voice anti-patterns ("never sound like...")

2. **Messaging framework:**
   - Core message: one sentence that captures the product's promise
   - Elevator pitch: 30-second version
   - Key messages by audience segment (if multiple)

3. **Content strategy:**
   - Content types needed (microcopy, onboarding, help text, marketing)
   - Tone variations by context (error messages vs. success states vs. marketing)
   - Content principles (concise over verbose, action-oriented, etc.)

4. **Naming conventions:**
   - Product terminology and glossary
   - Feature naming approach
   - UI label conventions

## Output

Content strategy, brand voice, and messaging framework documented. `step_2_complete: true`

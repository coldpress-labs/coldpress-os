## Goal

Establish the product vision section of the PRD — the "why" and "what" at the highest level.

## Instructions

1. **Product vision statement:**
   - Synthesize from product brief and context.md
   - One paragraph that captures the aspirational future state
   - "In [timeframe], [product] will [impact] for [users]"

2. **Product goals:**
   - 3-5 concrete goals for v1
   - Each goal should be measurable
   - Prioritize: must-have vs. nice-to-have

3. **Target users:**
   - Primary user persona(s) with key characteristics
   - Secondary users if applicable
   - User needs and pain points (from discovery)

4. **Success criteria:**
   - Quantitative metrics (adoption, engagement, retention, etc.)
   - Qualitative indicators (user satisfaction, ease of use)
   - Timeline for measuring success

5. **Scope boundaries:**
   - Explicitly in-scope for v1
   - Explicitly out-of-scope (future phases)
   - Known risks and assumptions

6. **Review with user** before proceeding.

## Output

Product vision section drafted and approved. `step_2_complete: true`

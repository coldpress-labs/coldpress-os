## Instructions

1. **Facilitate natural dialogue** — ask open-ended questions:
   - "What went well in this epic?"
   - "What was harder than expected?"
   - "What would you do differently next time?"
   - "Were there any surprises — good or bad?"
   - "How did the tooling and process serve you?"

2. **Listen and build on answers.** Don't rush through — genuine reflection takes time.

3. **Identify themes:** Process improvements, technical insights, planning accuracy, tool effectiveness.

4. **Document insights** as they emerge.

**Remember: NO BLAME. Focus on systems and processes, not individuals.**

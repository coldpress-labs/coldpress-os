## Goal

Define the visual direction including aesthetic approach, color strategy, typography, spacing, and design token foundations.

## Instructions

1. **Aesthetic direction:**
   - "What visual style fits this product?" (minimal, bold, playful, premium, etc.)
   - Reference existing products or designs that capture the right feel
   - Define the visual mood in 3-5 adjectives

2. **Color strategy:**
   - Primary, secondary, and accent color directions
   - Semantic colors (success, warning, error, info)
   - Light/dark mode considerations
   - Accessibility contrast requirements (WCAG AA minimum)

3. **Typography:**
   - Font family direction (sans-serif, serif, mono, variable)
   - Type scale approach (modular scale, custom)
   - Heading and body text hierarchy
   - Reading experience priorities

4. **Design tokens foundation:**
   - Spacing scale approach (4px base, 8px base, etc.)
   - Border radius strategy (sharp, slightly rounded, pill, mixed)
   - Shadow/elevation system
   - Motion/animation principles

5. **Inspiration references:**
   - 3-5 reference products or designs with notes on what to borrow
   - Anti-references: what to explicitly avoid

## Output

Visual direction and design token foundations documented. `step_3_complete: true`

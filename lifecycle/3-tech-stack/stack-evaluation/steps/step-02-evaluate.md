## Goal

Research viable technology options and compare them against the documented requirements.

## Instructions

1. **Identify candidates** — at least 2-3 viable options for the decision area.
2. **Research each option:**
   - Core features and capabilities
   - Community size and ecosystem maturity
   - Learning curve and documentation quality
   - Pricing model (especially free tier limits)
   - Known limitations and trade-offs
3. **Build comparison matrix** against the requirements from Step 1.
4. **Document findings** — append to the output document with sources where available.
5. **Flag any dealbreakers** — options that fail hard requirements.

## Output

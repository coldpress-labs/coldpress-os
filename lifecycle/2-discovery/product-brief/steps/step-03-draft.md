## Goal

Compose the 1-2 page executive product brief from discovery findings.

## Instructions

1. **Structure the brief:**
   - **Product Name & Tagline** — one-liner that captures the essence
   - **Problem Statement** — the pain point this product addresses
   - **Vision** — where this product goes (aspirational but grounded)
   - **Target Users** — primary and secondary user profiles
   - **Value Proposition** — why users choose this over alternatives
   - **Key Features** — 3-7 headline features (not exhaustive)
   - **Success Metrics** — measurable indicators of product success
   - **Strategic Context** — market positioning, competitive landscape summary
   - **Constraints & Assumptions** — known limitations and assumptions made

2. **Writing guidelines:**
   - Keep total length to 1-2 pages
   - Write for executive readability — no jargon, clear sentences
   - Every claim should be grounded in discovery findings
   - Use bullet points over paragraphs where possible

3. **Present draft to user** for initial feedback.

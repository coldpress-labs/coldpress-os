## Goal

Extract the rules and constraints that AI agents must follow when implementing this project.

## Instructions

1. **Technical constraints:**
   - Any required technologies or platforms?
   - Any banned technologies or approaches?
   - Performance requirements (speed, scale)?
   - Accessibility requirements?

2. **Business rules:**
   - Critical business logic that must be correct?
   - Regulatory or compliance requirements?
   - Data handling rules (privacy, retention)?

3. **Implementation patterns:**
   - Preferred coding patterns or conventions?
   - Existing design system or component library?
   - Testing expectations?

4. **Non-obvious rules:**
   - "What's something an AI agent might get wrong about this project?"
   - "What's the most important thing to NOT mess up?"
   - "Any past mistakes or gotchas to avoid?"

5. **Document findings.**

## User Interaction

This is the most important step — push for specifics. Non-obvious rules prevent AI implementation mistakes.

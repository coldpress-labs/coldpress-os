## Goal

Extract the project's core vision, goals, and scope from the user.

## Instructions

1. **Ask about the project:**
   - "What are you building?" (one sentence)
   - "Why does this need to exist?" (the problem it solves)
   - "What does success look like in 6 months?"

2. **Define scope boundaries:**
   - "What's definitely IN scope for v1?"
   - "What's explicitly OUT of scope?"
   - "Are there any hard deadlines?"

3. **Understand motivation:**
   - Personal project, startup, client work, internal tool?
   - Revenue model or purpose?
   - Scale expectations?

4. **Document findings** in output file.

## User Interaction

Conversational interview — ask questions one at a time, build on answers. Be a curious peer, not a form-filler.

## Output

Vision section documented. `step_1_complete: true`

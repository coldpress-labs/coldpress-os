## Goal

Build deep understanding of the target users through empathy exercises.

## Instructions

1. **Identify target users** — who are we designing for?
2. **Create empathy map** — What do they Think, Feel, Say, Do?
3. **Map pain points** — What frustrates them? Where do they struggle?
4. **Identify gains** — What do they want to achieve? What delights them?
5. **Build user persona(s)** if multiple user types exist.
6. **Document user journey** for the current experience (if applicable).

## User Interaction

Guide the user through empathy exercises with targeted questions. "Tell me about your users — who are they and what are they trying to accomplish?"

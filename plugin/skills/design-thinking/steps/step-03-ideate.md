## Goal

Generate a wide range of potential solutions.

## Instructions

1. **Load design-thinking methods** from data asset for ideation techniques.
2. **Generate solutions** using multiple techniques (Crazy 8s, brainwriting, analogies).
3. **Encourage wild ideas** — quantity over quality at this stage.
4. **Cluster and prioritize** solutions by impact vs. effort.
5. **Select top 2-3 concepts** for prototyping.

## User Interaction

Interactive ideation with the user, building on their ideas and introducing new angles.

## Output

Solution concepts documented and prioritized. `step_3_complete: true`

## Goal

Understand the presentation context before designing slides.

## Instructions

1. **Audience** — Who are they? What do they care about? Technical level?
2. **Core message** — What's the one takeaway?
3. **Constraints** — Time limit, format (in-person, virtual, async), number of slides.
4. **Call to action** — What should the audience do after?

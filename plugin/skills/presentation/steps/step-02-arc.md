## Goal

Structure the presentation for maximum impact.

## Instructions

1. **Opening hook** — How do we grab attention in the first 30 seconds?
2. **Build** — What's the logical progression of ideas?
3. **Key moments** — Where are the "aha" moments or emotional peaks?
4. **Resolution** — How does it all come together?
5. **Close** — Call to action, memorable ending.
6. **Present the arc** to user for approval.

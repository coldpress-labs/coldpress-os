## Goal

Turn opportunities into viable business model concepts.

## Instructions

1. **For top 2-3 opportunities**, sketch a business model canvas:
   - Value proposition, customer segments, channels
   - Revenue model, cost structure, key resources
   - Key activities, key partnerships
2. **Evaluate each model** for viability and alignment with user's capabilities.
3. **User selects** the most promising model(s).

## Output

Business model concepts documented. `step_3_complete: true`

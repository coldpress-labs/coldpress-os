## Goal

Surface opportunities that competitors are missing.

## Instructions

1. **Load innovation frameworks** from data asset.
2. **Apply Blue Ocean Strategy** — what can be eliminated, reduced, raised, created?
3. **Apply Jobs-to-be-Done** — what jobs are customers hiring products for?
4. **Identify unserved segments** or underserved needs.
5. **Prioritize opportunities** by market size × feasibility.

## Output

Opportunities identified and prioritized. `step_2_complete: true`

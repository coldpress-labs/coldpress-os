## Goal

Understand the competitive landscape before looking for opportunities.

## Instructions

1. **Define the market** — industry, segment, size, trends.
2. **Map competitors** — who else plays here, what do they offer?
3. **Identify current positioning** — where does the user's offering sit?
4. **Analyze trends** — what's changing in this space?
5. **Present landscape summary** to user.

## Goal

Understand what document this template produces and who uses it.

## Instructions

1. **Define purpose** — What document does this template produce?
2. **Identify audience** — Who fills it out? Who reads the result?
3. **Define category** — documents, infrastructure, wds, or contracts?
4. **Outline sections** — What sections are required?
5. **Review existing templates** for convention alignment.

Template design in frontmatter. `step_1_complete: true`
